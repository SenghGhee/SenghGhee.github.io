Emotional Intelligence (EQ)

Understanding EQ vs IQ and why it matters in software development.

What is Emotional Intelligence?

Emotional Intelligence (EQ) is the ability to notice, understand, and manage your own emotions, as well as navigate interactions with others. Rather than being a fixed trait, EQ is a collection of practical skills that can be developed over time through practice. It comes down to three main steps: practicing mindfulness, building self-knowledge/self-mastery, and creating useful mental habits.

How is EQ Different from IQ?

IQ (Intellectual Quotient) measures cognitive ability, raw logic, and problem-solving skills. While IQ is generally static, EQ measures emotional awareness, self-regulation, empathy, and communication. In coding, IQ helps you solve a complex logic bug, but EQ helps you handle the frustration when your code breaks, collaborate smoothly in a team, and stay calm under pressure.

Why EQ Matters

In tech, technical capability gets you through the door, but EQ is what determines long-term success. Key traits like initiative, adaptability, clear communication, and working constructively under stress all rely on EQ. For me personally, practicing mindfulness and self-regulation makes it much easier to step back from frustrating errors during study blocks, avoid mental fatigue, and keep learning effectively.
