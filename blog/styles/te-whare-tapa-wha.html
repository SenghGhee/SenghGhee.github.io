Te Whare Tapa Wha

Exploring holistic wellbeing through the four walls and foundation of Te Whare Tapa Wha.

Understanding the Model

Developed by Ta Mason Durie in 1984 Te Whare Tapa Whā models health as a wharenui (meeting house). If one wall is weak the entire house becomes unstable. To maintain balance during intensive learning in all four dimensions and the foundation beneath them need attention.

Why We Use It at Dev Academy

Dev Academy honours Te Tiriti o Waitangi by integrating Te Ao Maori perspectives into learning. Bootcamp coding is intense and tech isn't just about syntax it is about the whole person. Keeping all four walls strong prevents burnout and builds long term resilience as a developer.
